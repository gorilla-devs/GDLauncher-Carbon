## Installing this modpack

Click the search bar at the top of GDLauncher. Modpacks are shown by default. Paste the Quick Install ID from above or search for the modpack name, then click **Download**.

GDLauncher creates a new instance automatically, downloads everything, sets up the mod loader, configures Java, and allocates memory. Just wait for it to finish and hit Play.

## System requirements

Most modpacks need 6-8GB of RAM allocated to Minecraft and a few gigs of storage space. GDLauncher manages Java for you, so don't worry about that.
