## Installing this resource pack

Open an instance in GDLauncher (resource packs work with vanilla, no mods needed). Go to the **Addons** tab and click **Add Addons**. Select **Resource Packs** from the dropdown on the left of the search bar, then paste the Quick Install ID from above, find the resource pack, and click **Download**.

Once in-game, open Options → Resource Packs. You'll see your pack in the "Available" list on the left. Click the arrow to move it to "Selected" and hit Done.

## Stacking packs

You can use multiple resource packs at once. Packs higher in the list override textures from packs below them.

## Keep in mind

High-resolution packs (512x, 1024x) need more RAM and a decent GPU. Some packs also need OptiFine or the Continuity mod for connected textures to work.
