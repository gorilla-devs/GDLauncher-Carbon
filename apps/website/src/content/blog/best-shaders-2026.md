Shaders need either Iris (Fabric/Quilt/NeoForge) or OptiFine. GDLauncher's shader browser searches both Modrinth and CurseForge, see the [install-shaders guide](/guides/install-shaders).

Performance impact below is relative, every shader has presets, and your GPU matters far more than the shader name.

## Balanced shaders

Good visuals without melting your GPU.

### Complementary Reimagined

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Scalable (Potato → Ultra presets)
- **Style:** Realistic with stylized water and clouds

The most-downloaded shader pack on Modrinth. The Potato preset runs on low-end hardware; Ultra needs a decent GPU. There's also a sister pack, *Complementary Unbound*, with a slightly different look.

### BSL Shaders

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Medium
- **Style:** Vibrant, warm color grading

Long-running pack known for saturated colors and noticeable god rays. Heavily customizable.

### Photon Shaders

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Medium-High
- **Style:** Cinematic realism

Focuses on accurate atmospheric scattering, clouds, and reflections. Heavier than BSL but lighter than the high-end packs below.

## Performance-first shaders

For older hardware or maintaining high FPS on big servers.

### Complementary Reimagined (Potato preset)

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Very low
- **Style:** Subtle vanilla-plus

The Potato preset of the same pack listed above. Runs on integrated graphics while still adding waving plants, better water, and basic shadows.

### Tea Shaders

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Very low
- **Style:** Clean and minimal

Lightweight with surprisingly good water and lighting. No volumetrics, no path tracing, just a clean look.

### Sildur's Enhanced Default

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Very low
- **Style:** Enhanced vanilla

Keeps the vanilla look but adds waving foliage and improved water. Sildur also makes *Vibrant Shaders*, a heavier sibling.

### Super Duper Vanilla

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Low
- **Style:** Enhanced vanilla

Inspired by the cancelled "Super Duper Graphics Pack". Vanilla look with modern lighting touches.

## High-end shaders

For powerful GPUs. Expect a real FPS hit.

### SEUS PTGI

- **Compatibility:** OptiFine only
- **Performance:** Heavy
- **Style:** Path-traced realism

Software-based path tracing, no RTX hardware required, but you'll want a strong NVIDIA card. The PTGI builds are released through the developer's Patreon; the older free SEUS Renewed is still available on the official site.

### Continuum

- **Compatibility:** OptiFine (Continuum RT also needs OptiFine)
- **Performance:** Heavy
- **Style:** Cinematic

Used a lot for Minecraft cinematics. The free version is on the website; *Continuum RT* with real ray tracing is paid.

### Kappa Shader

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Heavy
- **Style:** Realistic

By the developer of *RRe36's Shaders*. Detailed clouds, volumetric lighting, and good water. Free version available on Modrinth.

### Nostalgia Shader

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Medium-High
- **Style:** Classic shader look (2015-era)

Same developer as Kappa. Looks like older SEUS builds, warmer, less HDR-y. Good if you don't like the modern overprocessed shader look.

## Stylized shaders

For unique looks instead of realism.

### Rethinking Voxels

- **Compatibility:** Iris (preferred), OptiFine
- **Performance:** Medium-High
- **Style:** Voxel-based path tracing

Builds on Complementary Reimagined with added voxel-based light propagation. Distinctive blocky-but-pretty lighting.

### AstraLex Shaders

- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Performance:** Medium
- **Style:** Bloom-heavy, dreamy

Strong bloom, saturated colors, and atmospheric haze. Polarizing, you'll either love it or find it overdone.
