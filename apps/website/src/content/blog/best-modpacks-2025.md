Whether you're new to modded Minecraft or a seasoned veteran, there's a modpack for everyone. Here are the best modpacks to play in 2025, organized by category.

## Best Kitchen Sink Modpacks

Kitchen sink packs include a wide variety of mods with no specific theme - perfect for players who want options.

### All The Mods 9 (ATM9)

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1
- **Mod Loader:** NeoForge
- **Recommended RAM:** 8-12 GB

The latest in the legendary ATM series. Features hundreds of mods including Create, Mekanism, and new additions. The ATM Star progression adds goals for long-term play.

### Better Minecraft [PLUS]

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1
- **Mod Loader:** Forge/Fabric
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

An enhanced vanilla experience with new biomes, structures, mobs, and quality of life improvements. Great for players who want more content without overwhelming complexity.

### Prominence II

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1
- **Mod Loader:** Fabric
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

An RPG-focused modpack with custom quests, bosses, and progression. Features a unique skill system and challenging combat.

## Best Tech Modpacks

For players who love automation, machines, and complex systems.

### Create: Above and Beyond

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.18.2
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

A Create-focused modpack where you progress through ages of technology using only mechanical power. Beautiful and innovative.

### Vault Hunters

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.18.2
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 8 GB

Enter randomly generated vaults to collect artifacts and level up. Combines dungeon-crawling with tech mods. Made famous by Iskall85.

### FTB Skies

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.19.2
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

A skyblock modpack from the FTB team. Start on a small island and work your way up through tech tiers.

## Best Adventure Modpacks

For players who love exploration, combat, and adventure.

### RLCraft

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.12.2
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 4-6 GB

The infamous hardcore survival pack. Dragons roam the skies, thirst and temperature mechanics, and brutal difficulty. Not for the faint of heart!

### Cobblemon

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1
- **Mod Loader:** Fabric/Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 4-6 GB

Pokemon in Minecraft! Catch, train, and battle Pokemon in a seamlessly integrated experience.

### Medieval Minecraft

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.19.2
- **Mod Loader:** Fabric/Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

RPG progression with classes, skills, and medieval-themed content. Great world generation and dungeons.

## Best Expert Modpacks

Challenging packs with custom recipes and long progression. For experienced players only!

### GregTech: New Horizons (GTNH)

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.7.10
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

The ultimate expert pack. Thousands of hours of gameplay with heavily modified recipes. A true test of dedication.

### Nomifactory

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.12.2
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

A GregTech modpack with streamlined progression. Less grindy than GTNH while still being challenging.

### Create: Dreams & Desires

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1
- **Mod Loader:** Forge
- **Recommended RAM:** 6-8 GB

Expert-style progression using Create as the foundation. Custom recipes and unique challenges.

## Best Lightweight Modpacks

For players with older PCs or those who want a simpler experience.

### Fabulously Optimized

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.4
- **Mod Loader:** Fabric
- **Recommended RAM:** 2-4 GB

Performance optimization pack that matches OptiFine features. Great baseline for adding your own mods.

### Simply Optimized

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.4
- **Mod Loader:** Fabric
- **Recommended RAM:** 2-3 GB

Minimal performance modpack focused purely on FPS improvements without changing gameplay.
