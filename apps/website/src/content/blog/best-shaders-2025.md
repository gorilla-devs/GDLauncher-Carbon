Shaders can completely transform Minecraft's visuals. Here are the best shader packs for different needs and hardware capabilities.

## Best Overall Shaders

These shaders offer the best balance of visuals and performance.

### Complementary Reimagined

- **Performance Impact:** Medium
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Realistic with stylized touches

The most popular shader for good reason. Beautiful water, realistic lighting, and excellent performance. Multiple presets from Potato (low-end) to Ultra. Works with almost every hardware setup.

### BSL Shaders

- **Performance Impact:** Medium
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Vibrant and colorful

Known for its vibrant, saturated colors and smooth visuals. Great god rays and atmospheric effects. Highly customizable with many options.

### Photon Shader

- **Performance Impact:** Medium-High
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Cinematic realism

Focused on realistic lighting with excellent performance optimization. Great for screenshots and videos.

## Best Performance Shaders

For older hardware or players who want to maintain high FPS.

### Complementary Reimagined (Potato)

- **Performance Impact:** Very Low
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Subtle enhancements

The Potato preset of Complementary runs on almost anything while still improving the game's look. Perfect starting point for low-end systems.

### Tea Shaders

- **Performance Impact:** Very Low
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Clean and minimal

Extremely lightweight with surprising visual improvements. Great water and subtle lighting effects without hurting FPS.

### Sildur's Enhanced Default

- **Performance Impact:** Very Low
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Enhanced vanilla

Adds subtle improvements like waving plants and improved water while keeping the vanilla aesthetic. Almost no performance impact.

### Super Duper Vanilla

- **Performance Impact:** Low
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Enhanced vanilla

Designed to look like an upgraded vanilla Minecraft. Maintains the original art style while adding modern shader effects.

## Best High-End Shaders

For powerful PCs that can handle demanding graphics.

### SEUS PTGI

- **Performance Impact:** Extreme
- **Compatibility:** OptiFine
- **Style:** Photorealistic

Path-traced global illumination for the most realistic lighting in Minecraft. Requires RTX graphics card for decent performance. Absolutely stunning results.

### Continuum

- **Performance Impact:** Very High
- **Compatibility:** OptiFine
- **Style:** Cinematic realism

Cinema-quality rendering with physically accurate lighting. Popular for Minecraft cinematics and trailers.

### Kappa Shader

- **Performance Impact:** High
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Realistic with style

High-quality shader with extensive customization options. Great clouds, volumetric lighting, and water effects.

### Nostalgia Shader

- **Performance Impact:** Medium-High
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Classic shader look

Inspired by classic shaders like SEUS v10. Great for players who want that nostalgic 2015 shader aesthetic.

## Best Stylized Shaders

For unique visual styles beyond realistic rendering.

### Rethinking Voxels

- **Performance Impact:** Medium
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Ray-traced look

Voxel-based path tracing for a unique blocky ray-traced aesthetic. Works on non-RTX cards!

### AstraLex Shaders

- **Performance Impact:** Medium
- **Compatibility:** Iris, OptiFine
- **Style:** Dreamy and colorful

Unique colorful style with bloom and fantasy-like lighting. Great for creative worlds.
